Official sources (downloaded 2026-10-29):
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notoserifsc
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notoserifjp
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosansjp

Subsets are renamed Seria Serif/Sans SC/JP and instantiated at weight 400.
After editing invitation text, rerun scripts/build_fonts.py to include new glyphs.
